Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Jovia is not live yet. Right now joviahealth.com is a pre-launch site, and the only personal information we ask for is the email address you give us to join the waitlist. This policy explains how we handle that, and how we will handle information once Jovia Derm launches.

1. Introduction

Jovia Health (“Jovia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this privacy policy (this “Privacy Policy”). Please read it carefully to understand our policies and practices regarding your Personal Information (defined below). If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, your choice is not to use our Platform.

This Privacy Policy describes the types of Personal Information we may collect when you visit our website at joviahealth.com (our “Website”), join our waitlist, communicate with us, or, once available, use Jovia Derm and any related application or service (together, our “Platform”), and how we use, maintain, protect and disclose that information.

“Personal Information” means any information that identifies, relates to, describes, is reasonably capable of being associated with, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular individual. It does not include de-identified or aggregated information.

Jovia is not a medical group or a health care provider. We are a technology company building a Platform that will connect you with independent, licensed health care providers (each, a “Provider”) through affiliated, physician-owned medical practices (each, a “Practice”), who will provide any clinical services (the “Services”).

When this Privacy Policy applies

This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect:

  • on our Website and Platform, including through the waitlist form;
  • in email and other electronic messages between you and Jovia; and
  • when you contact us for support or with questions.

When it does not apply

It does not apply to information collected by any third party, including through any website, application or content that may link to or be accessible from our Website. Once the Platform launches, health information you provide to a Practice or Provider in connection with the Services will also be governed by the Practice’s Notice of Privacy Practices, as described there.

This Privacy Policy is incorporated into our Terms of Service, which also apply when you use our Platform.

2. Not intended for children

Our Website and Platform are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from anyone under 18, and children under 13 must not use or provide any information on the Website. If we learn that we have collected Personal Information from a child under 13 without verified parental consent, we will delete it. If you believe we might have information from or about a child, please contact us at privacy@joviahealth.com.

3. Information we collect and how we collect it

Information you provide to us today

  • Waitlist sign-ups. When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address you enter. This is the only form on the Website.
  • Correspondence. If you email us, we keep a record of your message, your email address and anything else you choose to include.

We do not currently ask for, and you should not send us, health information, photos of your skin, payment details or government identifiers through the Website or by email.

Information collected automatically

  • Server logs. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically receives technical information when you load a page, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the date and time of the request. This is used to deliver and secure the Website.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser sends a request, including your IP address, to Google’s servers.
  • Browser storage. After you join the waitlist, we save a small flag in your browser’s local storage so the page remembers that you have already signed up. It contains no Personal Information and is never sent to us.

We do not currently use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on the Website. If that changes, we will update this Privacy Policy before doing so and, where required by law, ask for your consent.

Information we will collect once the Platform launches

When Jovia Derm becomes available and you choose to use it, we and the Practices will need additional information to provide the Services, such as your name, date of birth, contact and shipping details, medical history, skin photos, and payment information. We will update this Privacy Policy, and you will be presented with it and the applicable Notice of Privacy Practices, before any of that information is collected.

4. How we use your Personal Information

We use the information we collect:

  • to add you to the waitlist and tell you when Jovia is available in your area;
  • to send you occasional product updates and early-access invitations related to Jovia;
  • to respond to your questions and requests;
  • to operate, maintain, secure and improve the Website, and to prevent spam and abuse;
  • to comply with legal obligations and to enforce our Terms of Service; and
  • for any other purpose we describe to you when you provide the information, or with your consent.

We do not sell your Personal Information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

5. Disclosure of your Personal Information

We do not share, sell or otherwise disclose your Personal Information for purposes other than those described in this Privacy Policy. We may disclose it:

  • To service providers who help us run the Website and process information on our behalf, under obligations to protect it. Today these are: GitHub (website hosting), FormSubmit (receives the waitlist form and delivers the email address to us), Google (font delivery), and our email provider;
  • In a business transfer, to a buyer or other successor in the event of a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, bankruptcy, or sale of all or part of our assets, in which case Personal Information we hold would be among the assets transferred;
  • For legal reasons, to comply with a law, court order, subpoena or other legal process; to respond to lawful requests from public authorities; to exercise or defend legal claims; or to protect the rights, property or safety of Jovia, our users or others; and
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We may use and disclose de-identified or aggregated information that does not identify any individual, such as the total number of waitlist sign-ups, without restriction.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Leave the waitlist. You can ask us to remove your email address at any time by writing to privacy@joviahealth.com. Any marketing email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.
  • Access, correction and deletion. You may ask to see, correct or delete the Personal Information we hold about you. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. We will not discriminate against you for exercising them.
  • Browser controls. You can clear local storage or block requests to third-party services through your browser settings. The Website will still work.

To make a request, email privacy@joviahealth.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

7. Who may use the Platform

Jovia operates subject to state and federal regulations, and when it launches the Platform and the Services will be available only to adults located in U.S. states where they are offered. We plan to launch in stages, and the waitlist helps us tell you when Jovia is available where you live.

8. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

Because we do not track you across websites or sell or share Personal Information for targeted advertising, the Website does not change its behavior in response to Do Not Track signals. If we ever begin activities that such signals are meant to address, we will honor Global Privacy Control signals as required by applicable law.

9. Data retention

We keep waitlist email addresses until Jovia launches in your area and you decide whether to use it, until you ask us to remove you, or until we decide not to proceed with the waitlist, whichever comes first. We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond and to keep business records.

10. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your Personal Information from accidental loss and from unauthorized access, use, alteration and disclosure. The Website is served only over encrypted HTTPS connections. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the security of information you send to us.

11. Third-party sites

Our Website may link to websites or services we do not own or control. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We will update this Privacy Policy as Jovia grows, and in particular before the Platform launches. We will post any changes on this page and update the “Last updated” date above. If we make material changes to how we treat your Personal Information, we will notify you by email or through a notice on the Website.

13. Contact us

If you have questions, concerns or requests about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, contact us at privacy@joviahealth.com. For anything else, you can reach us at hello@joviahealth.com.
