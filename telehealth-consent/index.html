Telehealth Consent

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Jovia is not live yet. Jovia is not yet providing care. This consent will apply when you begin a visit through the Jovia platform after launch, and you will be asked to review and accept it at that time. Joining the waitlist does not require you to agree to it.

This Telehealth Consent explains what it means to receive care from a licensed health care provider (a “Provider”) through the Jovia platform, operated by Jovia Health (“Jovia”). Providers practice through independent, physician-owned medical practices (each, a “Practice”). Please read it carefully, and ask any questions before you start a visit.

1. What telehealth is

Telehealth means the delivery of health care services using electronic communications instead of an in-person visit. With Jovia, care is expected to happen mostly asynchronously: you complete a medical questionnaire and upload photos of your skin, and your Provider reviews them, may message you with follow-up questions, and makes a treatment decision. Some visits may take place by secure messaging, phone or video. Your Provider may decide that a live visit is needed, or that telehealth is not right for you.

2. Expected benefits

  • Convenient access to a licensed Provider without travelling to an office.
  • Ongoing follow-up as your skin changes, so your treatment can be adjusted over time.
  • Prescriptions, when appropriate, sent to a pharmacy and delivered to you.

3. Possible risks

As with any medical care, there are risks. Risks specific to telehealth include:

  • Your Provider cannot physically examine you. Photos may be unclear, poorly lit or incomplete, which may not give your Provider enough information to make an appropriate decision, so you may be asked for more photos or referred for an in-person visit.
  • Evaluation or treatment could be delayed by technical problems with equipment, software or internet connections.
  • If information about your medical history, other medications or allergies is incomplete or inaccurate, it could lead to adverse drug interactions, allergic reactions or other errors in judgment.
  • Although we use security safeguards designed to protect your information, electronic systems can fail, which could result in a breach of the privacy of your health information.
  • Prescription skincare treatments can have side effects, such as irritation, dryness, redness, peeling, sun sensitivity or, rarely, allergic reactions. Your Provider will discuss the specific risks of any treatment they prescribe.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Provide complete, accurate and truthful information about your health, medical history, current medications, allergies, and whether you are pregnant, breastfeeding or planning a pregnancy, and keep it up to date.
  • Upload clear photos that accurately show your skin, and only photos of yourself.
  • Read the instructions and information provided with any medication, use it only as directed and only for yourself, and tell your Provider promptly about side effects or if your condition gets worse.
  • Keep a relationship with a primary care provider and seek in-person care when your Provider recommends it or when you need it.

5. Not for emergencies

Telehealth through Jovia is not for emergencies or urgent conditions. If you have a severe allergic reaction, difficulty breathing, swelling of the face, lips or throat, or any other medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room immediately.

6. Prescriptions

Your Provider will decide whether a prescription is appropriate based on their independent clinical judgment. A prescription is never guaranteed, and some medications cannot be prescribed by telehealth or may require additional steps such as lab work or an in-person visit. You may ask for any prescription to be sent to a pharmacy of your choice.

7. Privacy and records

Your health information will be protected as described in the Notice of Privacy Practices and kept in your medical record. Communications may be transmitted and stored electronically. You may request a copy of your records. Visits are not recorded by video or audio without your consent.

8. Eligibility and location

You must be at least 18 years old, and you must be physically located in a state where Jovia offers care and where your Provider is licensed at the time of your visit. You agree to tell us your accurate location.

9. Your rights

  • You may decline telehealth or withdraw this consent at any time, without affecting your right to future care or treatment, by contacting hello@joviahealth.com. Withdrawal does not affect care already provided.
  • You may ask questions about your care and request an in-person referral at any time.
  • Alternatives to telehealth, such as seeing a dermatologist or primary care provider in person, are available to you.
  • Some states have additional telehealth rules, and your Provider will follow the laws of the state where you are located.

10. Your consent

By accepting this consent in the Jovia platform before a visit, you confirm that you have read and understand this information, have had the opportunity to ask questions, and voluntarily consent to receive health care services by telehealth from a Provider. This consent applies to your first visit and to all later telehealth care through Jovia unless you withdraw it.
